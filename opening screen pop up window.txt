Opening Screen Text

At 3:47am, the records died.

Not the lights.
Not the internet.
The systems that proved identity, ownership, employment, and authority.

By morning, hospitals could not verify doctors. Banks could not verify accounts. Courts could not verify property. The city remained intact, but the truth behind it disappeared.

You are a Digital Records Officer in Central London. Your terminal is one of the few still running, but its database is corrupted. It remembers fragments: names, roles, districts, testimonies, contradictions.

People now come to you with claims.

“I am a doctor.”
“That house is mine.”
“I have access clearance.”
“She is lying.”

You cannot restore the old world.

You can only decide what becomes true next.
